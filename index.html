Brain Tumor Segmentation and 3D Reconstruction from MRI data

Our project focused on developing methods for brain tumor segmentation and 3D reconstruction using medical imaging datasets. We implemented a UNet-based model for segmentation and a DeepBet model for 3D reconstruction, exploring and comparing various techniques throughout the process, including algorithms like Marching Cubes. This work involved overcoming challenges common in medical imaging, such as varying slice densities and resolutions, by applying advanced preprocessing techniques and data augmentation to enhance model robustness.Drawing from the extensive literature on tumor segmentation, we also identified possible future directions for the project, such as exploring UNet++ variants and incorporating tumor classification based on segmented data.

Affiliation: Georgia Tech
Course: Introduction to Computer Vision
Status: Complete

Pulmonary Embolism Analysis

In this project, we processed electronic health records (EHR) data for over 2,000 patients, focusing on data cleaning and imputation to ensure accuracy and reliability. Outliers were identified and addressed using the Interquartile Range (IQR) method, with missing values filled using a KNN imputer. We applied advanced clustering techniques, including DBSCAN, Hierarchical Clustering, and K-Means, to group similar patients for targeted analysis. The effectiveness of clustering was evaluated using the Silhouette Score, leading to the identification of the top five features most critical to the clustering process and providing actionable insights for patient segmentation.

Status: Complete

Humans and Machines in Medical Image Processing

3D reconstruction techniques are commonly used to better model physics systems, particularly in the medical field. By taking two dimensional images and reconstructing them into vital organs, for example, medical professionals can better assess the health of their patients and provide the appropriate care. This paper investigates reconstruction of a plant using MRI scans, in hopes to further explore how important these technologies can be in the medical field. Image segmentation is also a vital part of medical imaging, with different segmentation techniques – and in particular deep learning with segmentation, very useful to diagnosis of various medical conditions. This paper will also investigate the use of deep learning techniques on segmentation of brain tumors.

Active Aerodynamics of Morphing Camber

Changing the amount of camber, or curvature, present in an airfoil is a powerful means of controlling the forces and moments that it generates under fluid flow. Furthermore, the control authority provided is significant even if only a modest portion of the airfoil chord has the ability to change camber. Under this project, a study was made with the application for a Horizontal Axis Wind Turbine (HAWT) to use the ability to dynamically change the blade camber with programmed servos to give max aerodynamic efficiency in different wind speeds.

Affiliation: National Institute of Engineering
Course: Final Year Project/Thesis (Major Project) @ National Institute of Engineering
Status: Complete

Low Speed Open Circuit Wind Tunnel
